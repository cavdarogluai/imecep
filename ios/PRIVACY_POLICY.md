# imeCep Privacy Policy

imeCep collects no personal data: no account, email or phone number, no ads or analytics.

Messages travel over Bluetooth between nearby devices and are stored only on your device; uninstalling deletes them.

Location permission is used for Bluetooth scanning and optional location channels and is never sent to our servers. Optional location channels may use third-party Nostr relays (via Tor) that we do not operate.

imeCep is not an official emergency service; call 112 in danger.
